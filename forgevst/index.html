Atmosphere · Vocal

Forge : Haze

Your vocal, turned into air.

  • A pad no library hasdrop in your vocal – Haze freezes its held vowels into a dreamy, transparent pad, made from your own voice
  • Always in key with your songHaze finds the key across lead and harmonies and tunes every vowel to its chord – or set the key yourself
  • Breathes with your trackdepth, slow motion, a shimmer of grains and a large, soft room; an LFO in step with your tempo opens the filter, breathes or wanders
  • Render it or play itdrag the pad onto a track at your tempo, or play chords from your keyboard – every key a frozen vowel at its pitch

Snare Roll · Generator

Forge : Anvil

Hit after hit, into the drop.

  • The snare roll you've been digging forat your tempo, in your length, with your own snare – ready to drag onto the track
  • Endless rolls with one-click magicthe Magic Cube builds a new pattern, volume curve and accents with every roll. Each roll has a number, so your favourite comes back
  • Your drop hits harderthe hits speed up to thirty-seconds, the pitch climbs, the low end clears – and the tail carries straight into the one
  • Humanized build-ups, out of the boxtiming and velocity vary like a drummer's hand, accents on the beat, softer hits sound darker

Reverse Swell · Vocal

Forge : Ember

Breathe in before the vocal.

  • Skip the flip, bounce, flipdrag the vocal in – Ember builds the reverse-reverb swell that rises into the first syllable
  • Endless swells with one-click magicthe Magic Cube rolls decay, pre-delay, damping and width – now and then with phaser or flanger on top
  • Goosebumps before the first wordthe first syllable is found for you, the swell flows seamlessly into its onset – 1 beat to 8 bars at your tempo
  • Always in key with your vocalbuilt from the first syllable itself – same pitch, same tone, a swell no one else has
