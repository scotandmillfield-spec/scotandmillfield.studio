Vintage · Effect

Forge :
Patina

Your track,
a few years older.

An insert effect that ages what you play: the pitch drifts like tape and rests in between, noise comes and goes with every note, the record crackles and rumbles, and a lo-fi stage takes away bits and bandwidth.

Factsheet

  • Drift tape-like pitch that rests and swings out now and then
  • Lo-Fi bits, sample rate, bandwidth and drive, with one mix
  • Noise follows every note and fades with it
  • Vinyl crackle, pops and the rumble of the turntable
  • 10 factory presets tape, cassette, vinyl, shellac, sampler, telephone
  • Insert mono or stereo, latency reported to your DAW
  • Same passage, same sound every bounce comes out identical

Reverse Swell · Vocal

Forge :
Ember

Breathe in
before the vocal.

Drop a vocal. Ember finds the first syllable, sends it backwards through a reverb and hands you the swell that rises into the voice – ending exactly where the vocal comes in.

Factsheet

  • Load WAV, AIFF or FLAC – drop it on the window
  • Auto syllable first syllable found for you, handles to adjust
  • 1 beat – 4 bars at your project tempo, sample-accurate
  • Reverb decay, pre-delay, damping, low and high cut, width
  • Phaser · Flanger to set the swell in motion
  • Magic Cube rolls a new reverb · 5 factory presets
  • 24-bit WAV drag onto the track, ends on the vocal onset

Riser · Generator

Forge :
Furnace

Hit the cube.
Get a riser.

A finished riser in one click – noise risers, sweeps and downlifters over 2 to 32 bars, locked to your project tempo, rendered as audio and dragged straight onto the track.

Factsheet

  • 2 – 32 bars riser or downlifter at your project tempo
  • Magic Mode one click rolls a complete riser
  • 12 sections noise, tone, filter, LFO, phaser, space, delay …
  • Curves start, end and shape for every moving target
  • Follows the song MIDI-triggered, ends exactly on the bar
  • 5 factory presets and your own
  • 24-bit WAV preview = export, sample by sample
