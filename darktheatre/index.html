The house lights have long since died. Dust hangs in the single shaft of light that still finds its way through the broken dome, and somewhere beneath the stage a mechanism begins to turn – slow, patient, older than anyone who ever sat in these seats.

A mask lies forgotten on the velvet of the front row. It is waiting for a face. (Beispieltext – der echte Text zu Akt I folgt aus eurem Konzept.)
