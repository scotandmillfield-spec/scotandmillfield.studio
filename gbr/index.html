2 New entry

Manual double-entry booking — for anything that doesn't run through the bank (cash, privately paid expenses, depreciation, corrections).

3 Journal

All bookings of the selected year (optionally one month), newest first. Locked entries can only be corrected by a reversal (Storno).

4 BWA

Revenue minus expenses, one line per account — the selected year next to the previous year.

5 Account balances

Debit / credit totals and balance per account, cumulative up to the end of the selected year / month — including bank and partner accounts.

6 Year-end PDF export

A PDF for the tax advisor (no software access needed): BWA, account balances and one depreciation schedule per asset — on the company letterhead. The journal is not included.

⚙ Your details

Your letterhead for every invoice — company, address, bank and tax details. Stored on the server (saved automatically as you type).

§ Chart of accounts (Kontenplan)

Your SKR-03-based accounts, used for bookings and the BWA. Add, rename or remove as needed.

§ Data

Journal, chart of accounts and letterhead are stored on the server. Singles, royalties, statements, invoices, GEMA statements and label sales reports still live only in this browser until they move to the server as well — keep a backup. The backup contains everything. Restoring a backup restores the browser data; the journal from a backup is taken over only into an empty server journal.

“Clear browser data” permanently deletes the singles, royalties, statements, invoice addresses, GEMA statements and label sales reports kept in this browser. Journal, chart of accounts and letterhead on the server are not touched.
